# CH07 EEG/ECG electrode and block-spec evidence intake

Date: 2026-09-28 UTC. Owner-facing acquisition and review plan for [CH07's paused topology gate](../reviews/CH07_GATE.md). This page specifies **what evidence to collect**, without asserting a product limit, accepting raw recordings into Git, or authorizing any body-connected stimulus. The published [electrode/AFE source review](../literature/biopotential/2026-09-25_STATE_OF_ART_REVIEW.md) separates fabricated circuits, other devices, simulated claims and electrode experiments. Its motion-artifact study reports contributions from skin contact and leads that should be separated by the fixture wherever possible.

## Collection record for each electrode and use condition

| Field | Record and reason | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Sensor and fixture identity | Electrode material/geometry/lot, placement and reference geometry, lead length/routing, skin/phantom method, contact pressure, hair/skin conditions, time since placement, temperature/humidity, wear age, channel and polarity. Record approved population/use strata and sample count without identities. | Sensors |
| Calibration/provenance | Instrument and electrode emulator identity, method/version, calibration date, stimulus amplitude, frequency grid, uncertainty, acquisition chain and software revision. Supply a checksum and restricted evidence identifier; raw individual recordings stay in an approved access system. | Sensors + metrology |
| Complex source spectra | At each frequency record **both legs**' complex `Z(f)` (real/imaginary Ω, sign convention and uncertainty), measurement reference and state. Cover the owner-approved EEG/ECG passbands, low-frequency/polarization region, 50/60 Hz, and relevant alias/interferer frequencies. Report contact/open/worn/moving distributions or bounded percentiles with sample counts; a single 50 Hz magnitude is insufficient. | Sensors + analog |
| Offset and artifact | Differential half-cell DC offset and drift across settling, wear, movement and temperature; synchronized motion/lead/contact observations, duration, return-to-baseline and dropout annotations. Characterize spectral and transient error separately from stationary random electronic noise. | Sensors + algorithms |
| Signal acceptance | Approved montage/biomarker, required frequency response, signal amplitude distribution, accepted loss/alias/noise/interference, clipping and recovery time, quality flag latency and sample/time policies per state. Name the operating scenario and the approving owner. | Product + algorithms |
| IC boundary | Selected rail and power/area envelope, input admittance versus frequency, bias/pad/ESD/mux leakage and capacitance over PVT and state, converter noise transfer/ENOB, line and rail coupling, gain and offset range, protection/lead-off states. Identify actual PDK, device and test conditions or mark missing. | Analog + PDK + power |
| Body-connected safety | Intended market/use claim, applied-part and fault-boundary review, authorized excitation/lead-off/reference drive policy, hardware-off/reset/fault behavior and independent qualified reviewer. Until approved, no drive/current value is inferred here or enabled by this model. | Product + safety |

## Execution and review sequence

1. Approve the electrode geometry, measurements and intended use strata before testing. Use calibrated phantom/bench fixtures for electrical characterization. Any human data collection follows the program's separate consent, privacy and safety process; this repository receives no personal/health recordings.
2. Preserve original timestamps, gain, filtering and reference paths; log stationary and representative movement/contact changes separately. Repeat across the chosen strata, physical samples and wear/temperature corners, with enough replicates for the owner's uncertainty and coverage decision. Do not assume the paper's electrode impedance is a worst-case bound for this product.
3. Compute derived, de-identified aggregate complex impedance and offset/artifact envelopes with units, sample counts, method, uncertainty and evidence class. Keep private raw evidence in an access-controlled store; publish only approved aggregate summaries and a checksum/owner/version reference as allowed by the [data policy](../governance/DATA_POLICY.md). No private URLs, patient identifiers or vendor files in Git.
4. Algorithms/product approve modality-specific quality and recovery limits; analog/power/PDK submit actual pad/rail/ADC constraints; safety independently approves the body-connected boundary. Re-run both the resistive baseline and complex sensitivity with **reviewed replacement inputs in a new evidence class and schema**. The present assumed-only program deliberately rejects relabeling fixture values as measured.
5. Review DC and AC transfer, mismatch/interference, electronic noise, polarization/motion, headroom/recovery, alias, safety and power **for the same conditions**. Compare DC-coupled, AC/servo and possible active-contact alternatives in a recorded ADR. Freeze the selected CH07 block specification only after owners accept the envelope; start CH08 afterward.

The quantitative spectrum model in [`ch07_complex_electrode.py`](../../models/python/ch07_complex_electrode.py) is useful for sensitivity checks and test-vector planning. It is a lumped passive RC example, not a fit to the collected spectra or a complete AFE. The intake list can be closed only with reviewed external evidence and an explicit gate decision.
