# Safety evidence and ownership

**Status:** No hazard review, optical exposure limit, electrode-drive limit, human study or regulatory classification is approved. Safety-related rows in the master specification are candidate controls and research requirements, not evidence of compliance. OI-005, OI-011 and OI-015 remain open.

Before an externally applied electrical or optical drive path is designed, the product and qualified safety owners must record the intended use and market, exposure geometry/conditions, applicable current standards, hazard analysis, misuse and single-fault conditions, and an independently enforced shutdown/current/exposure bound. Establish the verification method, instrument calibration, responsible reviewer and revision in a dated review. A firmware/software command alone does not supply an independent hardware safety limit. Keep personally identifiable physiology outside this public repo per `docs/governance/DATA_POLICY.md`.

Link future analyses to `state/REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.csv` and `state/RISK_REGISTER.md`; do not fill numerical limits from unrelated reference equipment or a generic online summary. CH11–CH12 and CH22–CH24 require quantitative review for optical and power-drive paths, with signoff much later.
