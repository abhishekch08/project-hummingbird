Date: 2026-09-25 UTC. Function: low-energy host control plus loss-accounted multimodal sample transport between a candidate nRF and an unselected sensor ASIC. ## Quantitative trade before candidate selection

Using the versioned CH04 report, `research_raw` has **1,553,120 framed B/s** and optional `research_fast_stress` has **15,553,920 framed B/s** under *CH04's different invented packet fields*. With a hypothetical 70% overall payload utilization and 1.2 design guard, minimum illustrative **single-bit-lane** SCK is `ceil(8 × framed_Bps × 1.2 / 0.7)`: **21,299,932 Hz** and **213,310,903 Hz** respectively. This is a sensitivity comparison, **not** an actual host requirement or silicon performance. CH06's new frame format must recompute from unframed CH04 sample-group bytes; do not add CH04's already counted header twice. Wake/CS latency, DMA set-up and service duty can dominate even where mean clock arithmetic passes.

| Candidate transport family | Analytical throughput implication | Power/area/latency | Reliability and decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repeated register reads over a low-clock control bus | For invented 8 MHz and 70% effective utilization, ≤700,000 B/s before a margin; below CH04 research average. | Per-transaction overhead and CPU/wake energy unknown; simplistic bit-rate ceiling only. | Suitable as a control path candidate, **not** proof of research raw streaming. |
| Bounded SPI bursts with DMA, command/status window and explicit credits | 16 MHz at 70% gives 1.4 MB/s, below CH04 research framed 1.553 MB/s; 32 MHz gives 2.8 MB/s before 20% margin/CS overhead. Fast stress 15.55 MB/s exceeds even invented 64 MHz at 70% (5.6 MB/s). | Faster clock may raise I/O, RAM, wake and RF contention power; no current or area evidence. | Conditionally study bounded frames/sequence/CRC/credits; physical max rate and selected pins unknown. |
| Shared mailbox/register plus asynchronous streaming FIFO | Separates control and streaming traffic; full-duplex wire does not double one-direction bandwidth. | SRAM, descriptor/pointer crossing, interrupt and power costs unknown. | Candidate only with atomic ownership, loss/gap status and read-acknowledge ordering; no OpenTitan IP selected. |

Illustrative CRC-32c costs 4 B per packet; a candidate 28 B header plus 4 B CRC around 1024 payload bytes gives `1024/1056 ≈ 96.97%` *protocol* payload fraction, **before** separately declared physical utilization/CS/poll/wake. CRC-16 could save 2 B but has different burst-error detection properties; no safety/security requirement authorizes choosing either. Payload rounding for short sensor records and sample-group timestamps, retransmission and FIFO stalls can alter the real fraction. V1/V2 mandate host-revision qualification before claiming any electrical throughput.

## Finding → implication → provisional action

| Finding | System implication | CH06 action / hold |
|---|---|---|
| CH04 mean research load exceeds invented 16 MHz single lane with guard; fast stress exceeds 64 MHz. | A clock label or finite FIFO cannot cure a sustained deficit. | Model frame overhead and credit/queue conservation; explicitly fail overloaded scenarios. Do not freeze SPI frequency or make the fast research stress a product mode. |
| Nordic V1 offers TX/RX DMA but V2 identifies revision-conditional errors. | Firmware assumptions and snapshot semantics may fail on selected silicon/driver revision. | Publish parser/CRC and negative tests; require confirmed part/revision and actual controller traces. |
| OpenTitan V3/V4 makes software register and FIFO side effects explicit. | Ambiguous W1C, partial time reads, retry ownership or unknown offsets can lose data even at high SCK. | Define atomic snapshots, reserved-bit errors, sticky overflow, monotonically sequenced frames and read-then-commit rules. |
| IETF S1 specifies a checksum, not trust. | A valid CRC can accompany malicious body-drive commands. | No unreviewed actuator register; security/authorization is a separate CH25 and safety-owner gate. |

Physical selection/review remains held until host die build/errata, ASIC pad/clock, measured usable throughput/energy/wake traces, buffer depth, approved modes and 64-bit timestamp semantics are available (`OI-001/002/006/010/013/015`). Re-survey if transport, selected die revision or product priority changes. **Survey gate: PASS_FOR_CANDIDATE_CH06_MODEL; FROZEN_ABI_AND_PHYSICAL_LINK_HOLD.**
