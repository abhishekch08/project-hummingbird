## First-principles behavior and quantitative planning anchors

In a *stationary, small-signal* state, let `H₊(f)` and `H₋(f)` describe the two source/pad loading transfers. Symmetric differential stimulus has transfer `(H₊ + H₋)/2`, and common mode converts to differential with `H₊ − H₋`. These are the CH07 complex-sweep relations. For time-varying contact, polarization and motion, one fixed `H(f)` no longer applies; a future model must define states, measured switching distributions and the associated transient/offset sources. Static input noise is distinct from deterministic pickup and contact artifacts.

After a selected gain/servo and analog anti-alias path, the ADC samples at `tₙ`, quantizes/clips to the **selected** full scale and filters/decimates with its actual response. Output validity must represent **both** analog overload recovery and converter/filter settling; calibration/lead-off/reset transitions need explicit timestamps and invalid-sample accounting. `f_alias = |f_in − k·f_s|` for a sinusoid folded to the first Nyquist interval means a 1.5 kHz spur at an illustrative 2 kSPS folds to 500 Hz *before whatever analog attenuation and converter filtering the chosen architecture supplies*. Sampling a clean low-rate waveform cannot prove that attenuation.

The CH07 synthetic cases are 1 kSPS EEG and 2 kSPS ECG; the CH02/CH07 candidate eight-channel all-identical probes imply **8,000 or 16,000 output sample events per second** if all eight used that profile. If a model instead emitted an event at each assumed modulator tick, CH07's hypothetical 256 kHz/512 kHz clocks would require **2.048 million or 4.096 million channel ticks per second**, 256 times the output count. These are *event counts, not measured simulator runtime, silicon power or an approved data path*. Model the selected decimator at the fidelity its verification question requires. For comparison only, V1's 3-period step response corresponds to 3 ms at 1 kSPS or 1.5 ms at 2 kSPS **if that specific TI filter were used at those rates**. Hummingbird has no selected ADC/decimator or recovery limit, so neither number enters a CH08 pass criterion.

## Representation trade: selection remains held

| Possible model | What it can resolve | Cost/dependency and failure mode | CH08 decision input |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discrete sampled Python reference or SystemVerilog RNM | Gain/quantization, deterministic sample timing, state/quality flags, register semantics; output-rate event count follows channels × sample rate. | A modulator-rate event model costs 256 times as many channel events under CH07's illustrative OSR; neither approach proves unmodeled continuous contact, alias/ESD, analog saturation dynamics or clock crossing. Fidelity depends on validated input-rate and transfer assumptions. | Use for independent numeric oracle and fast integration only after selecting continuous-to-discrete interface; qualify tool and seeds. |
| Continuous electrical Verilog-A/AMS | Source/contact, high-pass/servo, nonlinear clipping and analog transient interaction at chosen abstraction. | Solver tolerance/timestep and convergence cost depend on fastest modeled pole and discontinuities; actual runtime cannot be quoted without a simulator. No EDA license/tool presently selected. | Compare with measured spectra/step/overload and selected netlist; choose only when CH07 topology and tool support exist. |
| Hybrid analog front end + sampled converter/RNM | Physical source/AFE transients with digital quality/control and host integration; explicit bridge/sampling boundary. | Cross-domain event ordering, precision, resets, filter latency and portability need equivalence tests; no simple “fast and accurate” guarantee. | Likely a useful architecture candidate, **not selected**; benchmark the chosen EDA flow and approved edge cases. |
| Approved aggregate stimulus and restricted trace replay | Preserves real transient/offset distributions under recorded use conditions; exposes omissions in parametric models. | Requires qualified sensor recordings, calibration and privacy controls; replay does not extrapolate to PVT/fault corners or supply a physical circuit model. | Use de-identified approved aggregates or metadata/checksums in public Git; raw personal data remain restricted. |

No credible area/power/volume trade is derivable from an HDL choice. Such metrics come from the selected AFE, ADC, pads, rail and package in CH07/CH03/CH29. Simulation speed may guide *verification abstraction* only after accuracy against independent evidence is demonstrated.

## Requirement-linked future test obligations

| Stimulus and oracle | State/failure to expose | Source of acceptance limit |
|---|---|---|
| Complex two-leg sweeps, DC source bias and unequal 50/60 Hz common mode | Loading, phase, differential leakage, input impedance and full-chain CMRR, both settled and contact transitions | CH07 measured source/pad and approved `BIO-0003/0004/0007/0010/0011` |
| In-band physiological plus independent seeded stochastic electronic noise; separate motion, half-cell and line pickup | Integrated noise PSD/weighting and SNR without RSS of coherent artifacts; sample quality/dropout | Algorithm/source envelopes for `BIO-0005/0008/0015/0020` |
| Bounded electrode offset, gain change, step overload, reconnect and rail/brownout/reset | Clipping threshold, analog recovery **versus** filter-valid time, calibrated state and flagged/invalid words | Frozen topology/ADC and approved `BIO-0006/0009/0016/0020`, CH03 rail/CH05 timing |
| Tone/interferer near `f_s ± f_band`, modulator/decimator clocks and gain states | Analog stopband, decimator rejection, images, passband droop and ENOB/SNDR conditions | Approved `BIO-0010/0011/0013/0015`; selected physical filter/ADC |
| Open/short/contact change, calibration, standby/wake, simultaneous eight-channel sampling and host backpressure | Lead-off reporting and allowed stimulation policy, channel coherency, timestamps, loss/status conservation | Approved `BIO-0001/0017/0018/0021`, `SAFE-0001/0003/0005`, CH05/CH06 ABI |

Each future test requires a unit-bearing input record, independent expected result/tolerance, mode/corner, seed (when random), implementation revision and simulator log. Model against approved recorded distributions and selected hardware, with a separate null/invalid state. Never turn absence of a limit into a passing assertion; body-connected excitation remains disabled until reviewed.

## Recommendation, uncertainty and gate

**PREENTRY_SURVEY_READY / CH08_ENTRY_HOLD.** The source set is sufficient to define model fidelity questions and a test matrix. It is insufficient to select a CH08 analog/digital partition, write a credible channel implementation, or pass requirements because CH07 has not selected a topology or frozen electrode/quality/ADC/safety limits. Re-search the electrode and circuit literature and complete a condition-normalized trade after real CH07 evidence arrives; then commit the refreshed architecture/model selection *before* any behavioral implementation. This review changes no physical risk closure or chapter status.
